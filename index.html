04 / The person behind the code

Researcher.
Builder. Educator.

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Biology at Miami University, with a B.Sc. in Environmental Science from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology.

My work brings together biological questions, computational methods, and scientific communication. I build tools with traceable inputs, explicit assumptions, and outputs that other researchers can inspect and reproduce.

Beyond research, I teach physiology laboratory courses and help researchers use R for genetic data analysis through LAMBDA workshops. I also serve as Treasurer of the Miami Association of Graduate Students.
