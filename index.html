PROJECT 02Statistical reliability

snpflowR

When does a directional signal
become a misleading conclusion?

An R research package examining directional genetic differentiation from SNPs, with auditable calculations, explicit uncertainty, and simulations designed to expose failure.

A prospective study of 15,125 synthetic datasets found that none of four resampling methods passed the overall calibration screen. The failures are part of the result.

02 / Research perspective

Grounded in biology.
Driven by questions.

My doctoral research investigates species boundaries and gene flow in closely related salamanders, Ambystoma barbouri and A. texanum.

I combine population genomics, phylogenetics, spatial analysis, and comparative transcriptomics to understand how lineages diverge and remain connected.

Jezkova Lab · Miami University
01

Where are species boundaries?

Use genomic and phylogenetic evidence to investigate independently evolving lineages.

02

How does ancestry move?

Examine gene flow, population structure, and the geography connecting populations.

03

What changes in gene expression?

Study expression differences across species and tissues through comparative transcriptomics.

Looking ahead / Reliable AI for science

The same question runs through my interests in biological models and AI: does a model capture meaningful structure, and how would we know when it fails?
